Common questions

  • How do I start? Sign in with your phone number, open Your Doodhwala, and choose a dairyman near you. You can order once that's done.
  • How do I place an order? Tap New Order or the chat button, pick your quantity, and send. You can also set a daily order that sends itself each morning.
  • Where is my delivery? Open Track Delivery to see your dairyman on the map while he's on his round.
  • How am I billed? Orders add up into one monthly bill. Pay by UPI, card or net banking in the app, or with cash on delivery. Every bill can be downloaded as a PDF.
  • Something looks wrong on my bill. Message your dairyman in the app, or email us and we'll look into it.
  • I want to delete my account. Profile → Delete Account in the app, or use the link below.
